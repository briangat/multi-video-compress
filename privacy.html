Privacy Policy

Multi Video Compress TimeStamp · Effective October 5, 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

The short version

Multi Video Compress TimeStamp does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Your videos

Videos you choose are read and compressed on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. The app reads each video's recording date and time from the video file itself, only to show it and to draw the date and time stamp.

Photos access

The app asks permission to add items to your photo library so it can save finished videos and screenshots there. Videos you pick are chosen through Apple's photo picker, which gives the app only the videos you select.

Settings

Your preferences, such as the target size and stamp style, are stored only on your device.

No accounts, tracking or ads

There are no accounts or sign-ins. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and makes no network connections of its own.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Email

If you email us for support, we use your message and email address only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
